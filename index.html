About the Channel

Double the fluff,
double the chaos.

Welcome to our YouTube channel, where we document the daily life of a Shiba Inu named Baozi or Buzz. Baozi is a lively and adorable Shiba Inu. On our channel, you'll see heartwarming (W Speed ❤️‍acyl) moments of Baozi exploring new environments, learning new skills, and interacting with his family.

We hope these videos bring you joy and relaxation, while also helping more people understand the unique qualities of the Shiba Inu breed. Thank you for subscribing and supporting us. Let's follow Baozi on a journey filled with laughter and love! 🐾
